## baba
she was at stonewall. insane fem lesbian. she can literally brew a potion and transform people. y'all gotta give her more credit than you think. enough said.

## bebop
he's a regular at a fighting pit which is saying something, but i think that given all his trauma and stuff and him being a good robotic son for ms. shelly that he kind of wants to get away from the freak lifestyle even though it sort of chose him. i wouldn't say he's very queer-coded, like i could definitely see this guy with like a pretty robot princess or something, but not much more long-term.

## billy
he's too much of an asshole to seriously be with anyone, but that being said he kind of gives me bi punk vibes. not bi because he's legitimately bi, but just bi for the aesthetic; i don't think billy would seriously consider being with a guy. that being said, his in-game mechanic is getting smacked on the regular, and he has been shown to have interest in vyper with one of their voiceline exchanges, including allowing her to call him lash, so he's definitely some sort of insane freak.

## calico
she's literally some sort of international assassin, there is no way that being with her is not some kind of freaky stuff. i don't really know much about her, but she's gotta be bi in the sense that she wouldn't let anyone out-dom her. she's really scary, but she's kind of into high fashion and what not which i think detracts from it a little...not so much prim and proper vibe, but more like straight cut.

## celeste
very gaga bi queer icon vibes, and she is hella tatted up which is really cool and wears a pretty revealing fit since she's a sideshow performer. i think she trumps calico a little bit because she's the kind of woman who will intimidate you just by having her exist in the same room as you. amazing character.

## deadman danny
i genuinely think this guy has never felt the touch of another person and is more so moved by the every word of maximilian fairfax. that's gotta be a little gay, but otherwise he's the most maga vanilla forever alone type i've seen in my life.
