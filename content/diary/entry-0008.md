i'm over it, don't read too much into this.

it's nice to meet you again. i already know you but i'm going to play dumb because you're going to think that i'm weird. we've actually never met face to face until this point, but who am i to hide the fact that i already know you?

i've always had pretty poor vision if i'm being real. i couldn't see your eye color from afar, but now you're letting me stare into them as i meet you. i like how your hand feels better in real life. i remember your smell from a common room that we shared a long time ago, it's very unique to you. i can see the frizz in your hair a bit better from up close, but i wouldn't tell you that because that would upset me the tiniest bit too. the silhouette of your face is just about the same, and your nose doesn't really look that different from far away or up close. your lips were really tiny, but they're a bit more noticeable now though still thin. i know that you don't know me, and i'm willing to entertain the fact that you're meeting with me for the first time but i've met you many times.

in passing, while going to class, on a sunny day, while eating a meal, eating at the outfits you wear, the laughs you share, in a floor above you looking down, in a floor below you looking up. truth be told, i'm so fucking lonely. i remember the faces of people around me because i know what it's like to bleed into the background and feel like a nothing. i know you don't need me to help you and that you aren't lonely, but i only see the best parts of you. i go through your instagram photos. i pause the frames of your dumb story highlights. i check your tagged. i google your name. you're so so cool and i want to know everything about you. the most selfish part of me wants to devour you and keep you all to myself. 

my sentimental ass creates cravings of friendship and romance, but the truth is that i don't want that. i want you, i want to have lived your life, i want to know what it's like to be in your skin and perceive the world through your brown eyes, the shade of brown eyes that i want. i want to be avoidant like you and live in the moment, to have lived on opposite coasts and seen different trees, different friends. i want to be in your room and imbibe the smell that i can't get enough of. i want to have the privilege of neglecting everything because of how original and interesting and attractive and funny and caring of a person i am, archetypes of people you commonly encounter in life be damned. i want your breathy laugh, your dumb college memories, the friends you brought into your life, the clothes you wear, the hair you style, your sleepless nights, your favorite nightmares to ponder on.

i couldn't have you all to myself, and i know you know that it was all i wanted. dear god, the life i created for myself has been damningly unsatisfying. i don't ask that i get everything i want, but please just let me see them, feel them, live through them just one more time.

don't read too much into this. please don't. it was never meant to be, and all you can do is tear your flesh, be hot for nobody, care to just exist even if it's for the sake of still being here. i don't need you, and you don't need me either, but just please don't leave me. please. please.

it hurts, it hurts so much. it hurts, it hurts, it hurts. i couldn't tell from that far away that seeing life through you would hurt me so much, because i know that you stopped this for the best. i know that when you saw me kneeling in front of you that you saw a broken person that wouldn't let you heal. do you know how much that pisses me off? that i couldn't think of you as a villain or a monster that is giving me a reason to feel destroyed, but rather that you are probably one of the people that has been the most delicate with me and genuinely cared to not hurt me more? do you think that i cried in my floor that night because i couldn't have you, or because i knew that you knew that it just would never be?

i'm over it. i'm over it. i'm really not. i wish i couldn't feel, really. what i wanted so much is something that i now have, and the heartache that lets me see life in your skin is killing me, because i know that you're a good person. you did what was right for us. this us could never be a goddamn thing, and you knew that i was getting my hopes up over nothing, but you know what? i really, really, really want to hate you but i just can't. i can't.

i'll miss you. the you that i wanted to devour, the you that i thought unattainable. and now that i have seen what you really are, i know that i could never eat your heart. you are my worst nightmare, and thinking of you just makes me want to cry. i tremble. 

but don't read too much into this. you probably will never even read this. i know i'm scary, i know i'm crazy, but may we meet in another life where you are the one watching me and i'm the one confronted with a gut-wrenching choice. i wish i could say that i want to hurt you too, but i don't because the truth is that you never meant to hurt me. and that's what makes you beautiful, my angel. i wanted you all to myself.
