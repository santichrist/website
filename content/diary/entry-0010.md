# i'm not totally sure
i didn't know this until like a week later, but apparently my [diary entry #8](https://santichrist.com/diary/entry-0008/) became somewhat of a local overnight sensation when i wrote it. the story goes that a certain someone (hi!) found it, read it, got really freaked out, showed their friends in college (hi!), and then read it at least 10 times over the week. i won't lie, i also read it a lot of times to myself. at best, it was some good prose brought about from an emotionally charged situation. at worst, i was losing it. the truth is probably somewhere in the middle, but it's certainly moments like these that make me proud of the angsty writing of a young woman undergoing some nasty character development. what i'm less proud of is the fact that they all more or less avoid eye contact with me now...

as a brief aside because i'm not really sure where to write this, [diary entry #9](https://santichrist.com/diary/entry-0009/) will not be getting finished. i was told that it kind of sucked, but the way that i see it there was simply no creating a good sequel to the diary entry that came before it, so i guess i'm just gonna leave it at that.

# so what now?
so said angsty young woman was sitting in her room during a cold thanksgiving break, all alone in her thoughts. i decided to stay here for thanksgiving because i was originally making plans with some friends to travel to like pittsburgh or something, but then that got kind of derailed. a friend offered to invite me over to his house for thanksgiving in jersey, but i didn't really want to be the only one to go to a straight man's house with his vaguely conservative family while i'm sitting there as a symbol of the ehl-gee-bee-tee agenda in america. 

that got me thinking about just how much of a foil i am to my younger brother (he's 19 at the time of writing). he has been dating his girlfriend for the last like 2.5 years long distance ish, and boy are they seriously committed to each other. i'm talking like they call every single day for hours and go on the cutest dates ever when they visit each other. they both go to church and are fairly religious, not to mention that they are taking each other very seriously to the point where he gave her a promise ring. they post cute photos of each other on instagram, have introduced each other to their families, take the whole dynamic crazy seriously. they are also each other's first relationship.

it's funny how we are taking completely different approaches to life, and i could not want to be more different from him in my dating life because it actually baffles me whenever i hear of people that young getting engaged with each other and what not, which is not exactly what my brother is doing right now but it's more than obvious that they intend to stay with each other for like ever, at least for now (the irony). i wouldn't say that i'm on the completely opposite side of this spectrum, but i'm far enough to where it's genuinely not even close to being the same.
