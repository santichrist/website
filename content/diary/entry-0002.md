# i woke up today to my mom telling me "i told you so"
for whatever reason, she had developed this superstition of not wanting to go to medellín to get an oral surgery procedure done in the last few weeks because a supposed oracle child from some random place, who had predicted earthquakes in venezuela, said that one was bound to happen in colombia. "bullshit," i thought to myself, because in the same vein as supposed oracles who were able to predict things like winning sports teams and what not, there's bound to be a large sample of people all claiming to be oracles, and in the end we focus on the very small minority (if not single person) who was blessed by the laws of probability to be right. and who knows, it could be a case of latina mom superstition that made her not go to colombia recently, but i'd be damned if she weren't right and a devastating earthquake of magnitude 7.2 ended up killing over 100 people in the region.

i guess it sort of shocked me to my core
