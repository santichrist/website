# second music post within the week, let's be more original gang
and you know what? i will happily recognize that i'm not being very original with my posts, but i nonetheless feel the need to share these with the world out there. too often i feel like i find an absolute banger of a song on like spotify (i sometimes pirate my music if i'm feeling extra bad but don't tell anyone), but then i check out how many listens it has and it turns out to be wildly unpopular for whatever reason.

as a result, i've gathered all 3 of my readers here today to highlight some songs that i think are insanely underrated. all i ask is that you credit me if you add any of these to your playlists. it's hard being a niche and original mf nowadays!

if it wasn't obvious, feel free to click on the song names since they're links!

## [bassvictim - i like it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iroAkuPVSC4)
alright so i'm afraid we are starting off in pretty niche territory. this song has about 1.1 million plays in an artist that peaks in the 10 millions for most of their songs. i'm not gonna lie, i shamelessly shazam'd this song from a coworker who was playing it on their car on the way to a pickleball court from my summer internship, and it was honestly even better to listen to the song by way of him playing it on a shitty 2000s corolla speakers, though i'm pretty sure he installed a sub anyway. cool stuff, genuinely, i recommend this artist if you're at all into looking like a pretentious electronic fan with mystery to their character.

## [metallica - through the never](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cdru3Z_F_Sw)
from metallica's self-titled album, this song has about 80 million plays in an album where two songs have about 2 billion plays. i think that definitely qualifies for being underrated.

i will admit that i'm a pretty big sucker for catchy guitar riffs, and in this case they manage to simply use the exact same riff all song and it just doesn't get old. the song's lyrics by themselves are not very exciting, and from what i recall they delve into the endless human drive to seek out information from the universe, but i'm really evaluating it at its surface level and not doing it any justice. either way, it's sometimes better to just turn off your brain when you listen to thrash metal.

## [bladee - shadowface](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SlP5ILjf0UY)
bladee might genuinely be one of the most underlistened artists that i regularly listen to on spotify, and shadowface has about 4.3 million plays with his popular work being in the 20-50 mil play range. i think that the only reason this song has accumulated so many plays is because of the fact it's been out for 12 years, and you know what? it's easily my favorite of his sad songs. pretty classic heartbreak song and it really captures just how bitter emotional pain can be, and how you try to mask it to no avail with surface level shit that doesn't really matter in the end. particularly striking if, just like me, you're the kind of person to avoid introspection in the immediate aftermath of a letdown since you know that it's going to bring about really intense emotions. so emo, so edgy, so sick.

## [bersuit vergarabat - madre hay una sola](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0rTMzPrHul4)
alright, so i guarantee that you haven't heard of this artist unless you're a fan of latin american rock. i consider bersuit to be lowkey like one of the latin american contributions to the 2000s craze of rock. this song has about 8 million plays from an artist that peaks in the 100s of millions, so we'll count it. the song itself is about mother nature (the song's title translates to "there's only one mother") and protecting the environment, but it's so emotionally charged and frankly moving for what is otherwise a pretty simple message. that being said, the fact that they actually cared about environmentalism in the mid-2000s when everyone scoffed it off gives the band flair imo. i also like their mix of acoustic and electric guitar in the song, and i'm afraid i'm a sucker for that in any kind of song. check it out!!!

## [fakemink - baklava](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tFllObkwiVw)
i am a well known glazer of mink, and i'm not gonna lie this song is not even underrated like that, it's actually quite liked by people that are tapped into his music, but i wouldn't say that it's on the popular spectrum of things. it has about 6.3 million listens on spotify and fakemink caps out at the 100s of millions of plays. like i said, i really don't think it's that niche of a song, but it's particularly impactful to me because of all the different times of life that i can name where i've listened to this song, especially when you consider that it's just a simple heartbreak song. it's very cute and in classic mink fashion he slows down the song halfway through for that added emphasis and pain and emotion. or maybe he's just dragging the song because he's lazy, but either way i love love love this song.

## [$uicideboy$ - thorns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uuy9oJmfFK8)
believe it or not, i also glaze $b way too much, but i think that this song is genuinely such a gem. i wouldn't say it's underlistened per say since it has like 87 million plays, but it's certainly not one of their bigger songs since those cap out at over 400 mil plays usually. it's such a chiller of a song and very catchy, nothing much more to say but i love playing this song when i can feel the outside breeze on a summer evening.

i think this is a good place to leave this at. remember, you MUST credit me for putting you on (please).
