can you tell that i like fakemink yet? my introduction to him was by looking at an instagram note of a certain someone that i don't talk to anymore (i miss you, maybe in another life), but if nothing else i can thank him and instagram's development team for introducing me to an artist that i enjoy. fakemink is overly enigmatic sometimes, sure, but like i said in [my little analysis of his song london life](https://santichrist.com/music/londonlife/), i understand the need for the modern artist to be a little cryptic for the sake of aura farming for an audience of angsty young adults like me.

rather than doing that analysis nonsense again, i'd rather take the time to write about the events in my life that pop up in my head when i listen to this song. i've said this previously, and maybe the last sentence was self-explanatory, but i'm a little weird in the sense that i will associate the songs i listen to with a particular place and time in my head. it can be as specific as associating a song with 6:31 am outside of my algebra 2 classroom in high school, or as broad as associating a song with the nighttime of the spring 2026 semester...and in this case, i associate 51 ttashpel pony ave. with that exact place and time.
