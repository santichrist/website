quotes that i enjoyed, followed by a few sentences that sum up my reaction.

## [quote 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSWil4kOyq8)
> CHRIS: you ever feel like nothin' good was gonna happen to you?
> 
> PAULIE: yeah, and nothing did. so what?

not to be an existentialist bastard, but this is my favorite quote in the whole show. i think that we could all use a bit of paulie's stoicism during our darkest moments.

## [quote 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qsF-TYxUL-U)
> JANICE: what'd you do with him?
> 
> TONY: we buried him. on a hill. overlooking a little river. with pine cones all around.
> 
> JANICE: you did?
> 
> TONY: oh Jesus, what the fuck? what'd you care what we did we him?

don't ask questions you don't want to know the answer to. as a testament to my anxious personality, i often ask these questions way too much.

## [quote 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgBqTgwB01s)

the social need to put up appearances makes us say things we don't mean. i don't care about you; i do care about you.

## [quote 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ohscge4f1Ak)
