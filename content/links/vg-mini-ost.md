this is an ode to brief songs that appear in transient moments of videogames, which sometimes are a shame because they end up sounding really good but their composition never went beyond something like a brief 30 second loop.

## [pico's school - it's go time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TaKro9WUB3c)
this is from a 1999 flash game. i loved this game as a kid for how crude it's violence was. 

## [deadlock - street brawl item draft (time is running out)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o6yoSCiEwJ0)
love, love deadlock. stay tuned for a certain tier list that i will be making.

## [commando 2 - tunnel war](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eDXCEmvnME4)
from a chinese flash shooter game released in 2008. so smooth and serene for an otherwise violent game, totally caught me off guard when i first played this as a 6 year old.

## [commando 2 - karate master](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYXEHoAT5Po)
another smooth one from the same game. why were we making such bangers for web games? baffles me how talented they are at making throwaway bangers like this.

## [call of duty 4: modern warfare - main menu ost](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uamh1l_tmeI)
and yet another instance of a very relaxing theme on a violent game. i'm starting to think there's a trend here guys stay with me now. to be fair, this is a rearranged version of the game's main theme, but i really, really enjoy listening to it and reminiscing about how good the late 2000s gaming scene was.

## [call of duty: modern warfare 2 - boat chase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ns-MAji3yY)
you can really feel the thrill of the chase.

## [mortal kombat (1992) - the bridge (the pit)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tgWtxnyggOQ)
if you've ever played this game in a real life arcade, this is a scary ass stage, and the fatality is equally as humiliating because your character gets thrown off a really high bridge onto spikes awaiting below. straight malevolent as hell, it's so scary.
